# Enrollment And Fees

## How to enroll

1. Pick a course from the catalogue and check that the batch start date still shows
   as open on the website.
2. Fill the enrollment form at `https://example-academy.test/enroll` with your name,
   email, phone number and the course code.
3. Pay the fee using any of the accepted methods below. Enrollment is confirmed only
   after payment is received.
4. You receive a confirmation email within 2 business hours containing the Zoom link,
   the Slack invite and the first assignment.

## Accepted payment methods

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
- UPI and net banking for learners paying in INR
- PayPal for learners paying in USD
- Bank transfer for corporate and group enrollments, against an invoice

We do not accept cash, cryptocurrency or cheques.

## Installments

Courses priced above 150 USD can be paid in two equal installments. The first installment
is due before the first session and the second before the midpoint session of the course.
There is no interest or processing charge on installments. Access to recordings is paused
if the second installment is more than 7 days late.

Courses priced at or below 150 USD must be paid in full.

## Discounts

- **Early bird**: 15% off when you pay at least 21 days before the batch start date.
- **Alumni**: 20% off any second or later course.
- **Group**: 25% off per learner for groups of five or more enrolling together.
- **Student**: 30% off against a valid student ID, available on one course per learner.

Only one discount applies per enrollment; they do not stack. The largest eligible
discount is applied automatically at checkout.

## Corporate training

Private batches for a single organisation are available for all four courses, with the
syllabus adjusted to the team's stack. Corporate batches are quoted per cohort rather
than per learner and require a minimum of eight participants. Write to mukesh@gmail.com
with the team size and the target skills to get a quote.
