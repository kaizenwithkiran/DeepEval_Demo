# Refund And Transfer Policy

## Refund window

- **Full refund** if you cancel before the first live session of your batch.
- **75% refund** if you cancel after session 1 but before session 4.
- **50% refund** if you cancel after session 4 but before the midpoint session.
- **No refund** after the midpoint session of the course.

The midpoint session is session 18 for Playwright With TS, session 24 for Selenium With
Java, session 9 for API Testing With Postman And REST Assured, and session 12 for
LLM Evaluations For Testers.

## How to request a refund

Email mukesh@gmail.com from the address you enrolled with, stating the course code and
the reason. Refunds are approved or declined within 3 business days and the money reaches
the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days after approval. Refunds are always
issued to the original payment method; we cannot redirect a refund to a different account.

## What is not refundable

- Discounted group and corporate enrollments, which are governed by the signed agreement
  instead of this policy.
- Any course where completion certificate has already been issued.
- Payment gateway charges on PayPal transactions, which the gateway retains.

## Batch transfer

You may move to a later batch of the same course once, free of charge, as long as you
request it before the midpoint session. A second transfer costs 25 USD. Transfers to a
*different* course are treated as a cancellation plus a fresh enrollment, and the refund
table above applies to the original course.

## If we cancel or reschedule

If a batch is cancelled by us, every enrolled learner gets a full refund or a free
transfer to any other batch, whichever they prefer. If an individual session is
rescheduled, the recording and a make-up session are provided at no cost; rescheduling
a session is not grounds for a refund.

## Missed sessions

Recordings of every session are shared within 24 hours and your access to them is
lifetime, so missing a live session does not affect completion. Attendance is not
tracked and does not affect eligibility for the certificate.
