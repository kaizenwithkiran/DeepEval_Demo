# Support And Contact

## Who to contact

- **Course content, syllabus and enrollment questions** — mukesh@gmail.com
- **Payment, invoice and refund requests** — mukesh@gmail.com, with the course code
  in the subject line
- **Corporate and group training** — mukesh@gmail.com, with the team size and the
  target skills

Email is the only official support channel for anything involving money. We never ask
for your card details, CVV, OTP or passwords over email, chat or phone. Ignore and report
any message that does.

## Response times

- Email: within 1 business day
- Slack doubts during an active batch: within 1 business day
- Refund decisions: within 3 business days
- Certificate issue after capstone approval: within 7 business days

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding Indian national holidays.

## What you need before the first session

- A laptop with at least 8 GB RAM and 20 GB free disk space
- A stable internet connection, 10 Mbps or better for screen sharing
- Admin rights on the machine so you can install Node.js, Java, Docker and an IDE
- A GitHub account, used for assignment submissions
- Headphones with a microphone

A tablet or phone is not sufficient; every session involves writing and running code.

## Languages

All sessions are taught in English with occasional Hindi explanation. Course material,
code and assignments are in English only.

## Accessibility

Recordings have auto-generated English captions. If you need a transcript or any other
accommodation, write to mukesh@gmail.com before the batch starts and we will arrange it.
