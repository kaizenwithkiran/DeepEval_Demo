# Certification And Placement Support

## Certificate of completion

A course completion certificate is issued when you submit the capstone project and it is
reviewed by the instructor. Attendance is not a criterion — you can complete the course
entirely through recordings.

The certificate carries your name, the course name and code, the batch dates and a unique
verification ID. It is issued as a PDF within 7 business days of capstone approval, and
the verification ID can be checked by any employer at
`https://example-academy.test/verify`.

Certificates are not graded and do not carry a score. There is no expiry.

## If the capstone is not approved

The reviewer returns written feedback listing what is missing. You may resubmit twice
at no cost. There is no deadline for resubmission, because recording access is lifetime.

## Placement support

Placement support is advisory, not a guarantee. We do not promise a job, an interview
or a salary, and we are not a recruitment agency. What is included:

- **Resume review** — one written review per learner, returned within 5 business days.
- **LinkedIn profile review** — one written review per learner.
- **Mock interviews** — two for Playwright With TS, three for Selenium With Java, and
  one each for the API and LLM evaluation courses. Each is 45 minutes with written
  feedback, conducted in the final month of the course.
- **Referral pool** — alumni who complete the capstone are added to a pool that is shared
  with partner companies once a quarter. Being in the pool does not guarantee contact.
- **Interview question bank** — a maintained set of questions asked in recent interviews
  for each tool, updated every quarter.

## Alumni access

Alumni keep their Slack access after the batch ends and can join any future batch's
weekly doubt-clearing call. Alumni also get 20% off any further course.
