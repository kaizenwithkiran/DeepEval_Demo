## How sessions run

Sessions are live on Zoom. The link is fixed for the whole batch, so one calendar invite
covers every session. Each session is recorded and the recording is posted to the Slack
channel within 24 hours. Recordings never expire.

A typical session is 60% live coding, 25% explanation and 15% questions. The last
15 minutes of every session are reserved for doubts.

## Breaks and holidays

There are no sessions on 1st January, and each batch has a one-week break at its
midpoint. Indian national holidays falling on a session day are announced in Slack at
least 3 days in advance and the session is moved to the next available day.

## Doubt support

- **Slack channel** — the primary support channel, answered within one business day.
- **Weekly doubt-clearing call** — every Saturday at 11:00 AM IST, open to learners of
  all active batches, not recorded.
- **One-on-one review** — 30 minutes per learner for the capstone project, booked through
  the link in Slack.

## Switching timings

If the timing of your batch stops working for you, you can move to any other running or
upcoming batch of the same course once, free of charge. See the transfer rules in the
refund and transfer policy.
